# Personas

Three students shape GRANTLY. The demo student on the landing page is Priya. Stories using these personas are in [USER_STORIES.md](USER_STORIES.md).

## Priya, undergraduate B.Tech

- **Profile:** OBC category, Madhya Pradesh, family income 2.4 lakh a year. Uses a mid-range Android phone on mobile data.
- **Goals:** find scholarships she qualifies for, know the next step, and not miss a deadline.
- **Frustrations:** long lists with no sign of eligibility, rules that assume she knows terms like "domicile", portals that need a laptop.
- **Features that help:** onboarding in four steps, ranked matches, Eligible and Needs information results with reasons, What-if simulator, mobile layout, offline use.

## Arjun, diploma student

- **Profile:** first generation learner, shares a laptop with family, prefers Hindi.
- **Goals:** understand the options in his language, learn the application steps, keep his data private on a shared device.
- **Frustrations:** English-only portals, jargon, fear of making a mistake, no account he can trust.
- **Features that help:** Hindi interface, glossary and Help page, step checklist, official links only, no account or tracking, export and clear data, keyboard support.

## Meera, postgraduate researcher

- **Profile:** applies to several fellowships at once, each with different documents and dates.
- **Goals:** keep deadlines and documents organised, compare awards, track every application.
- **Frustrations:** scattered spreadsheets, expired certificates found at the last minute, no single view of progress.
- **Features that help:** filters and sorting, Compare, tracker board with notes and history, document expiry and readiness, deadline calendar with `.ics` export and reminders, JSON and CSV export.
